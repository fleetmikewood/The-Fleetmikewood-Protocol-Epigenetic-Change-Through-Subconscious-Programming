# The Fleetmikewood Protocol

**The Hidden Script Running in the Background**  
**Reinterpreting Belief So Life Has a Chance**  
**Epigenetic Change Through Subconscious Programming**

---

For generations, many of us received the same quiet formula in the most suggestible state of the day—the hypnagogic window just before sleep:

> “Now I lay me down to sleep… if I die before I wake…”

The issue was never the existence of God or the act of prayer. It was the interpretive frame that repeatedly paired the divine with mortality threat, externalized control, and passive surrender. Over time, that framing can shape a psychological baseline of reduced agency and a subtle acceptance that the future of life ultimately rests outside our responsibility.

When that orientation becomes cultural default, it carries real consequences. It can lower the felt urgency of long-term stewardship of health, systems, and the continuity of life itself. What should feel intolerable—mutual vulnerability at extinction scale and large-scale physical loss—can begin to feel familiar.

This does not require abandoning God or tradition. It requires changing the interpretation.

### What the Fleetmikewood Protocol is
An open framework that treats the daily receptive window as informational infrastructure. Through deliberately designed scripts called **Designer Prayers**, it uses subconscious programming to support **epigenetic-level change**—influencing stress physiology, autonomic tone, gene expression patterns, and the biological baseline we carry and pass forward. 

It is a practical tool for helping mankind move forward by replacing inherited patterns of passive fatalism with conditioning that strengthens agency, resilience, healing, and long-term responsibility for the continuity of life.

### What it is not
It is **not** about rewriting DNA sequence.  
The focus has always been epigenetic and informational.

### Example Designer Prayer
> God, please upgrade my healing so I become my best self and for extreme longevity.  
> Thank you for my existence.  
> Amen.

### Purpose
The aim is not rejection of belief. It is reinterpretation: keeping the relationship with God while shifting the daily programming so that it actually gives the future of life a chance.

We can keep running the inherited interpretive default.  
Or we can choose the version that aligns belief with the continuity and flourishing of life.
